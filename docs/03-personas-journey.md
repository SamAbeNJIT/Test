# 3. Personas & Journey Map

## 3.1 Personas

### Sam Rivera: real-estate agent (primary)
- **Needs:** 25 "Open House" yard signs, fast and cheap.
- **Tech comfort:** high on phone, low with design tools. Has a logo PNG from Canva.
- **Goals:** know the price immediately, order on mobile between showings, get signs before the weekend.
- **Frustrations:** print shops that say "email us for a quote", surprise shipping fees, not knowing if the file will look blurry.

### Linda Okafor: café owner (secondary)
- **Needs:** one A-frame sidewalk sign and window decals with opening hours.
- **Tech comfort:** low. Has no design file, only an idea.
- **Goals:** someone to make it look right, not be charged for something ugly.
- **Frustrations:** jargon ("bleed", "CMYK", "vector"), fear of ordering the wrong size.

## 3.2 Journey map (Sam)

| Stage | Doing | Thinking | Feeling | Design response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Googles "cheap yard signs fast" | "Will this be a quote form?" | 😐 Skeptical | Promo bar + hero promise a date and free shipping right away |
| Choose | Picks 24 × 18, taps 25 | "Is 25 worth it vs 10?" | 🙂 In control | Save % and per-unit price on every quantity row |
| Upload | Drops Canva PNG | "Will it be blurry?" | 😟 Unsure | Live preview + "Our designers check every file before your proof" |
| Pay | Fills short form / Apple Pay | "Hidden fees?" | 😐 Cautious | Free shipping + free proof lines; "not charged until you approve" |
| Wait | Gets email in ~4h | "Did they get it right?" | 🙂 Reassured | Confirmation timeline with exact delivery date |
| Review | Opens proof, asks for bigger phone number | "Will changes cost me?" | 😀 Relieved | Unlimited free changes; version history |
| Commit | Approves | "Point of no return" | 😀 Confident | Two-step confirm that states the charge and that changes are no longer possible |
| Receive | Signs arrive Thursday | "Next open house I'll reorder" | 😀 Loyal | Reorder in one click (future feature) |

**Moments of truth:** price reveal (Choose), file anxiety (Upload), commitment (Commit). The design puts the most reassurance at each of these three points.
