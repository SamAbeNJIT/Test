# 4. Design Rationale: Nielsen's 10 Heuristics

How each heuristic shows up in Sticker Mule and in the Postline prototype.

| # | Heuristic | Sticker Mule | Postline prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visibility of system status | Live total + "$1.20 / sticker"; "Next: upload artwork →" under Continue; proof status emails | 4-step progress bar; confirmation timeline; proof status pill ("Waiting on you", "Approved") |
| 2 | Match with the real world | Sizes in inches, plain words | Real sign sizes (24 × 18 in, 6 × 3 ft), real materials (.040 aluminum, 13 oz vinyl), dimension lines on the proof |
| 3 | User control & freedom | Unlimited proof changes, cancel before approval | Edit/Remove in cart, Back to options, Go back on approval confirm |
| 4 | Consistency & standards | Orange = commit (Continue, Checkout), blue = explore/input (Shop now, Choose file) | Same split: yellow commits, blue explores, text links for secondary actions |
| 5 | Error prevention | Prepress team fixes files; proof before print | Two-step approval confirm; designers check every file; approval deadline stated up front |
| 6 | Recognition rather than recall | Radio lists with prices instead of dropdowns | All sizes and quantities visible with totals; summary repeats the choice |
| 7 | Flexibility & efficiency | Custom size/quantity, editable cart qty, Share your cart, Generate with Grok, skip upload | Custom size/quantity, editable cart qty, Share your cart, free design request, skip upload, express pay |
| 8 | Aesthetic & minimalist design | White space, big photos, short copy | One idea per section, product mockups instead of stock photos |
| 9 | Help users recover from errors | Plain support copy | Inline errors that say how to fix the problem ("Enter a 5-digit ZIP.") with focus on the first error |
| 10 | Help & documentation | "Size help" link right beside "Select a size" | Size help with a sign rule of thumb + 3 FAQs placed at the moment of doubt |

## Other principles used
- **Feed-forward:** "Next: upload artwork →" says what Continue does before you click.
- **Hick's Law:** at most 6 options per list.
- **Fitts's Law:** full-width primary buttons on mobile; large radio rows (whole row is clickable).
- **Loss aversion / risk reversal:** "Pay nothing until you approve" turns the purchase into a free trial.
- **Anchoring:** the 1-unit price is listed first, so the Save % on bigger quantities feels like a good deal.
- **Peak-end rule:** the proof approval (peak) and the delivery date confirmation (end) are the most polished moments.
